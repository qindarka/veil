# Echoes of the Veil — Story Bible

This is the writers-room document. `shared/storyData.ts` is the playable text;
this file is the *why* behind it, plus everything you need to extend the
campaign without breaking its heart.

---

## 1. World overview

### The Veil

The Veil is one long dream, still being dreamed. Long ago — before "ago" meant
anything — a being now called the **First Dreamer** found itself alone in the
empty dark and said: *let there be somewhere, so the dark can have someone in
it.* The Veil is that somewhere: a harbor moored to the sky, a city sung out of
crystal, a drowned library, a forest that walks, a lake that pays attention,
all of it one exhaled breath from a sleeping chest.

The Veil is not a place dreams *visit*; it is the dream other dreams pass
through. Sleepers from the waking world fall upward into it nightly and forget
it by breakfast. The ones who don't forget — the ones the Veil *calls* — are
**Veilwalkers**. The players.

Three physical laws worth knowing:

1. **The Veil runs on attention.** Things that are looked at, sung to, and
   remembered grow brighter and more solid. Things ignored fray. This is why
   lanterns matter, why the Loom weaves, why a party of 6–10 friends paying
   attention together is, in-fiction, a literal super-weapon.
2. **The Veil keeps receipts.** Promises, debts, kept memories and small
   kindnesses persist physically (the Loom, the glass pebbles, the wood's debt
   to the Thornwalker). Nothing loved is lost; it is *filed*.
3. **The Veil leaves room.** Every pattern — the Loom's cloth, the Library
   Oath's signature column, the orrery — has deliberate empty space in it for
   what might still return. Hope, expressed as upholstery.

### The First Dreamer

The sleeper at the center. Vast, kind, very tired. They dreamed the Veil as
*company* — that is the secret at the bottom of the Memory Pool — and they made
the Echoes so the dream could tend itself while they held it. Their tragedy is
structural: **every dreamer wakes.** Everyone they make mornings for, the
morning takes. An eternity of guests and no way to follow them home.

The Dreamer is never a damsel and never a villain. They are a person who has
been strong for too long without ever once being comforted.

### The Hush — grief calcified (NOT a villain)

The single most important canon rule: **the Hush is not evil, and it is not
attacking.** When you cry in a dream, the tears flow to the Mirrormere — that
is healthy. But when you refuse to cry at all, for ages, the unwept grief goes
somewhere too. It goes *quiet*. The Hush is the Dreamer's own unexpressed
grief, calcified into a stillness that drinks song and color — not out of
hunger, but because it is trying to **carry the unbearable so its maker never
has to.** It mutes what hurts. It scrapes the painful figure gently off the
mural. It kneels by the Dreamer and keeps the worst things safe (it guards the
sound of the Dreamer's *laugh* inside itself — see the Veilseer's gift).

The Hush "wins" not by conquering but by *finishing* — the way a person
finishes crying when they are certain no one is coming. The party's job is to
come.

Mechanically the Hush appears as: gray soundless districts, hush-frost,
hush-glassed wood, the shard in Caelis, tension-mood random events, and a
speaker whose dialogue is hushed stage-direction fragments:

> ( the light bends away. something vast declines to breathe. )

Write the Hush in lowercase parentheticals, present tense, no names, gaps
where verbs should be. It never threatens. It waits, counts, keeps, declines.

## 2. The six locations

### The Skyharbor (`skyharbor`) — Act 1 hub
- **Sense of place:** a wooden harbor moored to nothing, gold lanterns against
  a violet cloud-sea, warm boards, wind that smells of un-invented rain. The
  whole island breathes — one slow rise and fall per minute.
- **History:** the first place the Dreamer made after Serai, built as a
  crossroads so visiting dreams would always find a lit window. Every road in
  the Veil starts here, which is why every portal returns here.
- **Secrets:** the harbor's breathing syncs with the Heart's pulse (its
  slowing is foreshadowing). The mural's scraped-away seventh figure is the
  Hush — removed *gently*, by the Dreamer, the way you take down a photograph
  that hurts. The clapperless bell rings on true things; it rings by itself
  (random event), at the Awakening, and once at the very end.
- **Set pieces:** Serai at the great lamp, the Loom of Echoes (campaign
  status / the Oath), three sky-glyphs (first co-op ritual), mural, bell,
  five portals, the Lumenkeeper's hidden cache.

### Caelis, the Crystal City (`caelis`) — Act 2: SOUND
- **Sense of place:** violet crystal spires you can *hear* — every street a
  chord, the city one patient piece of music, with gray glassy districts where
  the music has been drunk away.
- **History:** Aurel sang Caelis up from raw light and taught it its chord.
  The city had no ruler — only the Listening Throne, a seat for whoever most
  needed to be heard that day. The Hush came through mid-song.
- **Secrets:** the gray districts spell an absence with edges — the swallowed
  sound is being *kept*, not destroyed. The fountain runs with light from the
  Heart, and its stutter is the Dreamer's tiring pulse. The shard is Aurel's
  own grief specifically.
- **Set pieces:** Aurel rebuilding, three resonance crystals (co-op), the
  Hush-shard shrine (moral choice: cleanse vs shatter), throne, fountain,
  hidden shrines for Songweaver (an unfinished chord in a wall) and Embersmith
  (the cold dream-forge).

### The Sunken Archive (`sunken-archive`) — Act 2: MEMORY
- **Sense of place:** a library the size of a sunken city, green-gold water
  that politely declines to drown you, shafts of falling light, thousands of
  memory-jars glowing like a city seen from a night ship.
- **History:** every memory the waking world loses washes down here; Thessaly
  files it. The Library Oath: *we keep what the waking lose, against the
  morning they want it back.* When the Hush flooded in, Thessaly sealed the
  stacks with three many-handed glyph-locks, locking herself out of her own
  life's work.
- **Secrets:** the shelving is sorted by *ache* (gentle losses near the
  surface). The orrery was built in the first morning with the dark already at
  its center — proof the Veil was dreamed *around* a sorrow, not invaded by
  one. Somewhere in the stacks is the jar holding Thessaly's own waking life,
  and she no longer remembers which.
- **Set pieces:** Thessaly on her rounds, three glyph-locks (co-op), the
  Memory Pool (moral choice: read vs seal — the Dreamer's first memory),
  tablet, orrery, hidden shrines for Chronicler (dream-script that writes
  back) and Tidecaller (the deep's kept pearl).

### The Wandering Wood (`wandering-wood`) — Act 2: GROWTH
- **Sense of place:** a forest that is, very slowly, going somewhere — paths
  reshuffle politely, lantern-fruit sways, the canopy keeps checking the same
  patch of sky. Petal-fall as weather.
- **History:** the wood used to fruit out of season whenever the Dreamer
  laughed. No laughter in a long while, so it walks a patient search pattern,
  looking. Rowan planted the Heartwood — two saplings twisted into one trunk —
  with a waking-world someone who never came back.
- **Secrets:** the wood wanders partly so the gray can't *find* the rest of
  it; standing still for the party is an act of trust. The oldest stump is a
  calendar (this year's ring shows new green — the wood heard the party
  coming). Rowan has stood at the thorn-line for ten years, not choosing.
- **Set pieces:** Rowan at the waystone, three waystones (co-op), the
  Heartwood (moral choice: prune vs nurture), the stump, hidden shrines for
  Thornwalker (the green door, the debt repaid) and Warden (the hungry
  guardian-stone).

### The Mirrormere (`mirrormere`) — Act 2 optional: REFLECTION
- **Sense of place:** a lake so still it embarrasses the sky; reflections
  arrive a half-step late and sometimes wave first; glass-pebble shore; a
  silence that is *listening* (explicitly unlike the Hush).
- **History:** the Dreamer cannot bear to look at themselves anymore, so the
  Reflection does the looking and keeps it gentle. Tears cried in dreams flow
  here; the lake polishes kept moments into glass pebbles — the Dreamer's
  favorite shelf.
- **Secrets:** the mere is how the Dreamer watches the party (the first thing
  they have looked at in a hundred years). The Reflection collects answers to
  one question: *why did you come?* The Still Pool "forgets to reflect and
  shows instead."
- **Set pieces:** the Reflection, the Still Pool (boon choice: gaze vs offer),
  the glass shore, hidden shrine for Veilseer (walking *into* the Hush and
  finding the Dreamer's laugh kept safe inside).

### The Heart of the Veil (`heart-of-the-veil`) — Act 3
- **Sense of place:** down the pale stair, past the roots of every saved
  place, into a hollow vaster than sky where the dream wears thin as breath on
  glass. The waking world is audible through the floor, like a street heard
  from a warm bed.
- **History/secrets:** the Dreamer sleeps on a dais of woven light, curled
  around a small dark star — the core of their grief, held the way you hold
  what you can't look at and can't put down. The Hush stands everywhere,
  motionless: not a garrison, a vigil. Every pulse in the Veil (fountain,
  harbor-breath) originates here, and the gaps between beats are lengthening.
- **Set pieces:** the Dreamer (two communion beats), five heart-glyphs (final
  co-op ritual, every hand needed), the great Loom (THE ending choice).

## 3. Character sheets

Format: **voice / want / wound / relationship arc.** Relationship scores live
in campaign state (`relationships`, npcId → number) and are moved by
`relationship` effects; high totals are referenced in epilogue prose and are
the table's emotional scoreboard.

### Serai, the Lantern-Bearer (`serai`, gold `#ffd27a`)
- **Voice:** warm, steady, ceremonial without stiffness; speaks in light
  metaphors; memorizes faces aloud. Laughs "like a struck bell."
- **Want:** for no one to ever again arrive in the dark.
- **Wound:** she was the *first* made — she has watched every other Echo's
  place fall to the Hush while she could only keep lamps against it. Survivor
  of a slow catastrophe, armed with a wick-trimmer.
- **Arc:** greeter → quest-giver → oath-witness → (epilogues) either released
  into every future lamplighter (wake), keeper of lights that never go out
  (mend), or teacher of the new keepers' welcome (become). Gains relationship
  from her intro beat, the Lumen gift, the complete chord, and the afterglow.

### Aurel, the Crystalwright (`aurel`, violet `#8d7bff`)
- **Voice:** craftsman's clip — short sentences, inventories, "mind the
  lintel." Grief managed through productivity. Conducts when overwhelmed.
- **Want:** the city's whole song back; to stop hearing the silence.
- **Wound:** he was conducting when the Hush drank the city mid-song. He has
  built nonstop ever since *so he would not have to grieve* — the shard in the
  plaza is literally his unshed part of the Hush.
- **Arc:** deflection → confession (focus text) → the shard choice puts his
  grief in the party's hands. Cleanse: he stands in the current of every lost
  sound and comes out whole (+2). Shatter: freed fast, minus small precious
  things; he forgives the party before he forgives himself (−1). Either way
  the Echo is restored — the *texture* differs, and the epilogues remember.

### Thessaly, the Archivist (`thessaly`, teal `#4be3c3`)
- **Voice:** dry, precise, affectionately bossy ("Mind the third shelf. It
  bites."). Weeps with excellent posture. Deflects with cataloguing jokes.
- **Want:** for nothing true to be lost — her "whole faith" is knowing.
- **Wound:** double-locked. She sealed her own library to save it (keeper of a
  closed book); and she long ago filed her own waking life jar by jar so she'd
  never miss it — and no longer remembers which jar.
- **Arc:** the Memory Pool choice tests her faith either way: *read* honors
  truth and gives the campaign its biggest revelation (+1, she finally knows);
  *seal* honors the limits of knowing (+2, the party models the mercy she
  could never grant herself). Future content hook: finding her jar.

### Rowan, Warden of the Wood (`rowan`, spring `#9fe060`)
- **Voice:** plainspoken, gruff-gentle, farm-cadence ("Could use hands. Could
  use hands that mean it."). Pats trees like oxen. Jokes are rare and dry.
- **Want:** the Heartwood safe; the wood's search ended; the laughter back.
- **Wound:** the Heartwood is a promise planted with someone who woke and
  never returned. Rowan has stood at the thorn-line for ten years "not
  choosing" — and knows now that not-choosing is also a choice.
- **Arc:** the Heartwood choice is Rowan's therapy by proxy. *Prune:* Rowan
  makes the cut themself, steady at last (+1; "some healing looks like loss
  for a while"). *Nurture:* Rowan crosses the thorn-line and adds their hands
  — the risk rewarded with no-name colors (+2). The wood plants instead of
  searching forever after.

### The Reflection (`reflection`, pale blue `#a9c8ff`)
- **Voice:** still water — slow turns of *your* voices and faces; speaks
  mostly in questions; collects answers. Kind, uncanny, never coy about
  important things.
- **Want:** to keep the Dreamer's looking gentle; to gather proof (the party's
  answers) that coming back is something people do.
- **Wound:** it *is* a wound, structurally — the self-image of someone who
  cannot bear to look at themselves. Its one unguarded moment (focus text) is
  wearing its own face: the Dreamer's, young and hopeful.
- **Arc:** explainer of the Hush (the "where does the water go?" speech is the
  campaign's thesis statement) → Still Pool boon-giver (+2) → in `become`
  epilogues, colleague of the new keepers.

### The First Dreamer (`dreamer`, dawn-gold `#ffe9b3`)
- **Voice:** vast, slow, gentle; thunder from a sky already moved on; speaks
  without waking; tender hyperspecificity ("one very sarcastic librarian").
- **Want:** surface: rest. Truly: *to trust someone with the thing they
  carry* — they hand the party their literal thread.
- **Wound:** the founding loneliness ("let there be somewhere, so the dark can
  have someone in it") compounded by eternal hosting: every dreamer wakes;
  they never learned to grieve, so the grief went quiet and grew.
- **Arc:** felt presence (acts 1–2: the breathing harbor, the stuttering
  fountain, the watching mere) → confession → total trust. The ending choice
  is the party answering the Reflection's question — *what will you do after
  you've saved everything?* — with the Dreamer's own life as the loom.

### The Hush (`hush`, dusk violet `#7d739c`)
- **Voice:** lowercase stage directions in parentheses; gaps; never threats.
  ( finally someone strong enough to take this from me. )
- **Want:** to keep the unbearable so its maker never carries it alone — and,
  unspeakably, to be *relieved of the duty*.
- **Wound:** it is one — the Dreamer's, externalized.
- **Arc:** ambient dread (gray districts, frost, surge events) → faced at the
  shard (its grief given to the party's judgment) → seen truly in the act-3
  ritual focus text (a kneeling keeper, not a monster) → resolved by every
  ending: dissolved into ordinary tears (wake), falling as rain at last
  (mend), or rendered obsolete by a circle with no gaps (become). The Hush is
  never fought and never dies; it is *completed*.

## 4. The eight archetypes — narrative hooks

Each archetype has exactly one attuned interactable (a private beat: item +
lore + relationship bump + `gift-<archetype>` flag, all referenced by the
epilogues). Design intent: in a 6–10 player party, *every single player* gets
one moment that is unmistakably, only theirs. Write these beats in second
person singular and make the world recognize the player's backstory.

| Archetype | Hook object | The moment | Item |
|---|---|---|---|
| Lumenkeeper | `sky_hidden_lumen` | The cache of First Light: every lightkeeper before you added a little; you add, you take, the dark thins | `first-light` 🏮 |
| Songweaver | `cae_hidden_songweaver` | A lute fused into a wall, three notes from resolving for centuries; you finish it and the city's chord makes room for one more voice | `silent-chord` 🎼 |
| Embersmith | `cae_hidden_embersmith` | The cold dream-forge and the key abandoned mid-make; your family's trade — one true strike finishes it | `ember-key` 🗝️ |
| Chronicler | `arc_hidden_chronicler` | The dream-script blur resolves into a letter addressed to you-by-trade: *it is us, writing back. keep carrying them.* | `unwritten-page` 📖 |
| Tidecaller | `arc_hidden_tidecaller` | Below the lowest shelf, the deep returns what it kept: a pearl with a lullaby inside. "This is the receipt." | `tide-pearl` 🦪 |
| Thornwalker | `wood_hidden_thornwalker` | The hedge opens a door on no map; the wood pays its debt of paths with a charm grown for you specifically | `thorn-charm` 🌿 |
| Dreamwarden | `wood_hidden_warden` | A guardian-stone that held its watch alone for centuries — kin — and you feed its ward back to full flame | `ward-knot` 🪢 |
| Veilseer | `mir_hidden_veilseer` | You walk *into* the Hush and find the Dreamer's laugh kept safe inside; the Hush lets you, because you never flinched | `hush-glass` 🔮 |

Forward hooks if you extend the campaign: the Lumen cache implies prior
walker-generations; the Chronicler's correspondent is an open mystery; the
Veilseer is the natural protagonist of any Hush-sympathetic side story.

## 5. Act-by-act walkthrough & branch map

### Act 1 — The Skyharbor (~20 min)

```
enterLocation skyharbor ─► a1-arrival
interact sky_echo_serai ─► a1-serai            (clue: wake the glyphs)
sky_lore_mural / sky_lore_bell / sky_loom ─► lore beats (optional)
sky_hidden_lumen ─► gift-lumen                 (Lumenkeeper only)
allInteract sky_glyph_1..3 (60s) ─► a1-awakening
    sets `awakened`, unlocks caelis/sunken-archive/wandering-wood/mirrormere,
    setAct 2, mood hopeful, triggers a1-oath (+6s)
a1-oath ─► GROUP CHOICE `oath` (60s):
    remember | shelter | mend  →  flag choice:oath:<option>
```

The Oath is a pure tone-setter: no mechanical gate ever reads it, but **every
ending has one epilogue fragment per oath option** — the promise made in
minute fifteen is quoted back in minute ninety.

### Act 2 — The three Echoes (~45 min, any order) + Mirrormere

Identical skeleton per Echo location (Caelis shown):

```
enterLocation (when awakened) ─► arrival beat
interact npc ─► dialogue 1 (sets aurel-1) ─► dialogue 2 (needs aurel-1, sets
    aurel-2 + clue)            [focus text = the Echo's wound]
lore objects ─► optional lore beats
allInteract puzzle ×3 (30s) ─► sets <loc>-attuned, exposes the shrine
interact shrine (when attuned) ─► MORAL CHOICE (60s), mood tension
choiceResolved ─► one beat per option; BOTH set the echo flag, mood hopeful,
    give the location item to all, and move the Echo's relationship
```

| Location | Choice id | Options | Relationship | Shared flag | Item |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Caelis | `hush-shard` | cleanse / shatter | aurel +2 / −1 | `echo-caelis` | `shard-tear` |
| Archive | `memory-pool` | read / seal | thessaly +1 / +2 | `echo-archive` | `memory-drop` |
| Wood | `heartwood` | prune / nurture | rowan +1 / +2 | `echo-wood` | `heartwood-seed` |

Asymmetries that matter: *read* is the only option that changes act-3 texture
(the party witnesses the first memory — and the `become` ending's last
fragment quotes it back). *Cleanse* is the only negative-avoiding option whose
alternative carries a real cost (−1, lost sounds). Don't "fix" these; the
unevenness is the point.

Mirrormere (optional, no echo flag): reflection dialogue ×2 (the Hush thesis
speech), shore lore, then `still-pool` choice (gaze / offer, 45s) → single
resolution beat `mir-boon` (+2 reflection, `still-water` to all). The choice
flag `choice:still-pool:gaze` feeds a `become` epilogue fragment.

### The gate — "any 2 of 3"

`FlagExpr` can't count, so three `flags`-triggered beats (`a3-gate-ca`,
`a3-gate-cw`, `a3-gate-aw`) cover the echo pairs, all guarded by
`none: ['act3-open']` so exactly one fires. Effects: `act3-open`, unlock
heart-of-the-veil, setAct 3, mood tension. The prose explicitly invites
collecting the third Echo first; the `all-echoes` beat (`flags`, all three
echoes) rewards it with the Chord Complete moment and flips every
`all-echoes` epilogue fragment from bittersweet to triumphant.

### Act 3 — The Heart (~20 min)

```
enterLocation heart-of-the-veil (when act3-open) ─► a3-arrival
interact heart_dreamer ─► a3-dreamer-1 ─► a3-dreamer-2 (clue: the ritual)
allInteract heart_glyph_1..5 (90s) ─► a3-ritual (sets heart-ritual, climax)
interact heart_loom (when heart-ritual) ─► THE ENDING CHOICE `ending` (90s):
    wake | mend | become
choiceResolved ─► a3-wake / a3-mend / a3-become
    each: mood hopeful, journal, `ending` effect (ending-wake / ending-mend /
    ending-become), triggerBeat a3-afterglow (+9s)
a3-afterglow ─► the shared last beat (the bell rings once, clear and glad)
```

### Consequences → endings matrix

Every ending's epilogue is assembled from fragments whose `when` exprs read
the campaign's flags. Current matrix:

| Fragment condition | wake | mend | become |
|---|---|---|---|
| `all-echoes` set | nothing lost in the folding; Echoes released | seamless, gold-mended; age of tending begins | Echoes stand the watch with you |
| `all-echoes` NOT set | the morning is "a little thin"; room left | a lamp stays lit for whoever goes | first duty: bring the last one home |
| `choice:oath:remember` | "it was home", never "just a dream" | the Living Stacks | nothing loved filed under lost |
| `choice:oath:shelter` | the watch-fires never go out | lanterns at every threshold | you become Serai's welcome |
| `choice:oath:mend` | broken things gravitate to your hands | the oath woven into the selvage | maintainers at last |
| gentle choices (`cleanse`/`nurture`/`seal`, any) | — | the mend shines brightest there | — |
| clear-eyed choices (`read`/`gaze`, any) | — | — | you knew what you were inheriting |
| any `gift-*` flag | the gifts survive waking; "the Veil keeps its receipts" | — | — |

When adding content, prefer **adding fragments** over editing ending base
text — fragments are cheap, composable, and read aloud beautifully.

## 7. Themes & tone guardrails

**Themes, in priority order:**
1. *Grief must be witnessed, not defeated.* The Hush is completed, never
   killed. Every moral choice is a stance on how to treat pain.
2. *Attention is love is light.* Looking after things literally keeps them
   real. Co-op rituals = attention made mechanical.
3. *Nothing loved is lost — it is kept.* The Archive, the pebbles, the Loom's
   left room. Endings reframe loss as keeping.
4. *Together.* "Whatever you make, make it together" is the only instruction
   the first morning ever had.

**Tone guardrails (binding):**
- Mysterious + hopeful. Eerie moments are allowed — and must resolve toward
  warmth *within the same beat or event*. The hush-surge passes; the lanterns
  spite it. Never end a beat on dread.
- No villains, no combat, no corpses, no betrayal-by-NPC. Antagonism in this
  world is always grief, fear, or absence wearing a costume.
- The Dreamer is never pathetic; the Hush is never cruel; the Echoes are never
  damsels. Everyone is competent and tired, not helpless.
- Beats are read aloud at a table: 40–120 words, present tense for action,
  second person plural for the party ("you") and singular for archetype
  gifts. One image per sentence; cut adjectives before cutting images.
- Earn sentiment with specificity (a child practicing scales, wrong note and
  all) — never with abstractions ("the power of friendship").
- Humor is allowed and dry (Thessaly, the riddle merchant, "if trees could
  grin"). Never snarky about the emotions themselves.
- The waking world is never disparaged. Waking is morning, not exile; the
  tragedy is one-way doors, not "reality is gray."
- Choices must be dilemmas between two *goods* (truth/privacy,
  certainty/hope, quick mercy/slow mercy) — never good vs obviously-evil.

## 8. Extending the story (practical guide)

All campaign content lives in `shared/storyData.ts` (schema:
`shared/story.ts`). It is pure data — imported by both the client and the
Durable Object — so: no imports beyond types, no side effects, no DOM/three.
The flow: a trigger fires on the server → the StoryDirector broadcasts the
beat → effects apply in array order. Beats are once-per-campaign by default.

### Checklist for any new content
1. Object ids must exist in the canonical table in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`
   (and therefore in a location's interactables). New object = update the
   table + the location file first.
2. Gate dialogue chains with both `none: ['my-flag']` on the first beat and
   `all: ['my-flag'], none: ['my-flag-2']` on the next, so matching order
   never matters.
3. Every `triggerBeat` target should be `trigger: { type: 'manual' }`.
4. New flags: kebab-case; never reuse the canonical ones for new meanings.
5. Run `npx tsc --noEmit -p tsconfig.json` — the schema catches most slips —
   then re-grep your objectIds against ARCHITECTURE.md (a typo here silently
   breaks a puzzle at the table: the interact just whispers `denied`).

### Worked example: a new two-beat secret + choice in the Skyharbor

Suppose the location agent adds a lore object `sky_lore_figurehead` (a
ship's figurehead that washed up moored-to-nothing). Add to `beats`:

```ts
{
  id: 'a1-figurehead',
  act: 1,
  title: 'The Figurehead',
  location: 'skyharbor',
  trigger: { type: 'interact', objectId: 'sky_lore_figurehead' },
  when: { none: ['figurehead-found'] },          // first-look beat
  text:
    'A ship’s figurehead leans against the quay — a carved swan, eyes shut, ' +
    'sea-worn smooth. No ship in the cloud-sea ever carried her. She is ' +
    'humming, very softly, a harbor song with no harbor left.',
  effects: [
    { type: 'setFlag', flag: 'figurehead-found' },
    { type: 'journal', category: 'lore', title: 'The swan with no ship',
      text: 'A figurehead hums a harbor song with no harbor left.' },
  ],
},
{
  id: 'a1-figurehead-2',
  act: 1,
  title: 'The Swan Asks',
  location: 'skyharbor',
  speaker: 'serai',                               // resolved to name+color
  trigger: { type: 'interact', objectId: 'sky_lore_figurehead' },
  when: { all: ['figurehead-found', 'awakened'] },// 2nd look, post-ritual
  text:
    '“She sailed a dream that ended kindly,” Serai says. “She could rest — ' +
    'or watch the roads with me. She has been too shy to choose.”',
  choice: {
    id: 'figurehead',
    prompt: 'What does the party tell the swan?',
    durationMs: 45000,
    options: [
      { id: 'rest', label: 'Rest now',
        description: 'Her watch is over; let the harbor keep her song.' },
      { id: 'watch', label: 'Watch with Serai',
        description: 'Give her a new prow: the harbor itself.' },
    ],
  },
},
{
  id: 'a1-figurehead-rest',
  act: 1,
  title: 'The Song Kept',
  location: 'skyharbor',
  trigger: { type: 'choiceResolved', choiceId: 'figurehead', optionId: 'rest' },
  text: '…',                                      // 40–120 warm words
  effects: [{ type: 'relationship', npcId: 'serai', delta: 1 }],
},
// + a matching 'watch' beat. The flag choice:figurehead:watch now exists
// for free — reference it from an ending epilogue fragment:
//   { when: { all: ['choice:figurehead:watch'] }, text: '…the swan still
//     watches the roads…' }
```

That's the whole loop: object → beat(s) → optional choice → automatic
`choice:<id>:<option>` flag → epilogue fragment. No engine changes.

### Adding a whole location (summary)
1. Add the id to `LOCATION_IDS`/`LOCATION_NAMES` in `shared/constants.ts`.
2. Build the location class under `src/world/locations/` (extend
   `LocationBase`, register canonical-style interactables via the
   `Interactables.ts` factories) and register it in `WorldManager`.
3. Document its interactable ids in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.
4. Gate it behind a beat with an `unlockLocation` effect, give it an arrival
   beat (`enterLocation`, `when` your gate flag), a portal pair, and a
   `defaultMood`.
5. Write its content here first — sense of place, history, secret, the
   Echo's voice/want/wound — *then* write the beats. The bible is upstream of
   the data.
